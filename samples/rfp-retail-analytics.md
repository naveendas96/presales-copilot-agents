# RFP: Unified Analytics and AI Platform (Synthetic, Fictional Retailer "Northwind Mart")

## Background
Northwind Mart operates 220 stores and an e-commerce site. Data is siloed in an on-prem Oracle warehouse, SAP ERP and a Salesforce CRM.

## Requirements
1. The solution must ingest data from SAP ERP, Salesforce CRM and the Oracle warehouse on a daily batch schedule.
2. The platform must support near real-time ingestion of e-commerce clickstream events with latency under 5 minutes.
3. The vendor shall migrate 8 years of historical sales data from the Oracle warehouse.
4. The solution must provide a governed semantic layer with certified KPIs for finance and merchandising.
5. The platform must implement role-based access control and column-level masking of customer PII.
6. The solution should deliver 15 executive and operational dashboards.
7. The vendor shall build a demand forecasting machine learning model at store and SKU level.
8. The solution should provide a natural language assistant so business users can query sales data in plain English.
9. The platform must comply with data residency requirements and keep all data in-region.
10. The vendor must provide data quality monitoring with alerting on pipeline failures.
11. The vendor shall provide knowledge transfer and training for 40 internal users.
12. The solution must support disaster recovery with an RPO of 4 hours.

## Commercials
Proposals are due in 3 weeks. The client expects go-live of phase 1 within 6 months.
